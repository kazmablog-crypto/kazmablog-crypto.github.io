Privacy policy

Application: NCT komentare. Last updated 25 August 2026.

Who this covers

This application has exactly one user: the owner of the YouTube channel NOBODY COUNTED THIS. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

What data the application accesses

Using the Google account of the channel owner, and only after that owner has granted consent, the application accesses:

  • comment threads published on videos belonging to that channel, including the text of each comment and the public display name of its author, as YouTube already shows them publicly on the video page;
  • the ability to publish replies to those comment threads as the channel.

It does not access private messages, subscriber lists, e-mail addresses, payment information, or any Google service other than YouTube.

What the application does with that data

Comment text is used to draft replies and to identify recurring questions that may become the subject of a future video. Drafts are reviewed by the channel owner, who decides what is published. Nothing is published without that review.

Storage and retention

Comment text and the authorization token are stored in the channel owner's own working environment, for as long as they are needed to prepare and publish replies. The authorization can be revoked by the channel owner at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the application's access.

Sharing

No data obtained through the YouTube Data API is sold, rented, or shared with any third party, and none of it is used for advertising, profiling, or building audience segments. Comment text is processed by an AI assistant for the sole purpose of drafting replies for the channel owner's review.

Limited Use

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
