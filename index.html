A private tool for managing comments on the YouTube channel NOBODY COUNTED THIS.

What this application does. It reads the comments left on videos published by the YouTube channel NOBODY COUNTED THIS, and posts replies to those comments as that channel. It is used by one person: the owner of the channel. It is not offered to anyone else, has no sign-up, and has no users other than the channel owner.

How it works. The application uses the YouTube Data API v3 with a single authorization scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl, which is the scope YouTube requires in order to read comment threads and publish replies to them. Replies are drafted with the help of an AI assistant and are reviewed and approved by the channel owner before anything is published.
